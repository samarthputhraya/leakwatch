**Reading the table.** 2019 always has 10+ leaks flowing; the share of windows above τ grows through the year as leakage accumulates, so alerts are frequent and a random alert generator with the same number of alerts would also catch nearly every leak within 7 days (chance level). Precision and false alarms per week are the informative columns; the onset contrast shows how often a new abrupt leak visibly raises the score within 24 h.

**Target (PRD §10): at least half of the abrupt 2019 leaks with ≤ 2 false alarms per week.** Abrupt leaks detected and false alarms: main 9/9 at 2.90/week; strict 9/9 at 3.95/week; delta 9/9 at 4.56/week; residual 8/9 at 2.38/week; residual_ci 4/9 at 1.40/week. The target is not met by any variant at its 2018-tuned τ and m; the live model (residual_ci) misses it. The matched-false-alarm table in the DL report shows each detector's best 2019 operating point within 2 false alarms per week.

## 7. Limitations

- K-Means on window statistics sees the network's overall state; in a year where leaks keep accumulating (2019) the state drifts away from the training year and alerts saturate. A deployed system would re-baseline after repairs; this evaluation deliberately does not tune on 2019.
- Simulated scaling data come from the nominal model with ±10% demand noise; they test volume and throughput, not detection quality.
