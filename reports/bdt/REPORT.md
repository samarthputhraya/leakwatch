## 7. Limitations

- K-Means on window statistics sees the network's overall state; in a year where leaks keep accumulating (2019) the state drifts away from the training year and alerts saturate. A deployed system would re-baseline after repairs; this evaluation deliberately does not tune on 2019.
- Simulated scaling data come from the nominal model with ±10% demand noise; they test volume and throughput, not detection quality.
